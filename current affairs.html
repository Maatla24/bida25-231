National Stadium Erupts as Botswana Wins World Relays Gold

Published: MARCH 9, 2026

By: Maatla Francis

>GABORONE - In a historic moment for African athletics, Botswana's men's 4x400m relay team clinched gold yesterday at the Debswana World Athletics Relays Gaborone 26. Running in front of a sold-out home crowd, the team—anchored by world champion Collen Kebinatshipi and featuring Olympic icon Letsile Tebogo—clocked a staggering 2:54.47. The time is not only a national record but the third-fastest performance in world history, smashing the championship record by nearly three seconds. "It was not about the medals," Tebogo told reporters post-race, "it was about how this crowd held us together." The event marks a successful debut for Botswana as a host of the World Athletics Series, cementing the nation's status as a global sprinting powerhouse.

Bank of Botswana Delivers Aggressive 200-Basis-Point Rate Hike

Published: APRIL 22, 2026

By: Maatla Francis

FINANCE - The Bank of Botswana (BoB) has stunned markets by raising the benchmark lending rate from
3.5% to 5.5%. Governor Lesego Moseki announced the decision following an emergency Monetary Policy
Committee meeting, citing a "severe energy shock" triggered by ongoing geopolitical conflicts in the
Middle East. The hike is a preemptive strike against soaring inflation, which is projected to average 8.7% in 2026.
This move makes Botswana the first African nation to aggressively tighten its monetary policy in response
to the current global oil disruption. The central bank warned that while the move is necessary to stabilize
the Pula, it will likely dampen consumer spending and increase the cost of credit for local businesses through
the remainder of the year

President Boko Welcomes Rwanda's Paul Kagame for State Visit

Published: APRIL 20, 2026

By: Maatla Francis

DIPLOMACY - President Duma Gideon Boko is hosting Rwandan President Paul Kagame today for a high-stakes state
visit aimed at diversifying Botswana's economy. The visit follows the conclusion of the Botswana-Rwanda Joint
Permanent Commission of Cooperation (JPCC), where officials from both nations drafted agreements on digital
trade, tourism, and veterinary vaccine production. A key focus of the discussions is the formalization of a "double taxation agreement," which would ease the
flow of investment between Gaborone and Kigali. For President Boko, the partnership with Rwanda—an emerging
tech hub—is seen as a vital step in reducing Botswana's dependence on the diamond sector, which has faced
significant market volatility in the first quarter of 2026.
