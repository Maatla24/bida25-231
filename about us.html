ABOUT US

The Voice of Botswana

Who We Are

Founded in 2025, 267News was established to bridge the digital information gap in Botswana. Named after our nation's international calling code, we pride ourselves on being the most reliable source for local breaking news, health updates, and political insights.

Our Mission

To provide every Motswana with accurate, real-time, and unbiased news that empowers the community and encourages national dialogue.

Our Vision

To become the leading digital news authority in Southern Africa, recognized for journalistic integrity and innovation.

Why Choose 267News?

In a world of "fake news," we prioritize fact-checking and on-the-ground reporting. From the streets of Gaborone to the furthest reaches of the Okavango, we go where the story is.
