STUDENT REVOLUTION

BY 267NEWS Staff Gaborone, Botswana | APRIL 15, 2026


UB students strike after the Ministry of Road and Transport increased transport fares by 75%. This is a major concern as students struggle to sustain themselves on the P1900 allowance from DTEF. These funds cover food, rent, hygiene, and transport.

The catalyst was the unfulfilled promise by the UDC government to increase allowances to P2500. Following the protests, the Minister of Higher Education increased the allowance to P2200 and reduced fares, proving the power of a united youth voice.
